Do’s (Follow these practices):

✔️ Use strong, unique passwords for each account (combine letters, numbers, symbols).
✔️ Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on email, social media, and banking apps.
✔️ Verify links before clicking—especially from unknown emails, WhatsApp, or SMS.
✔️ Check UPI requests carefully—remember, you don’t need to enter a PIN to receive money.
✔️ Use official apps only (like bank apps from Play Store/App Store) for payments.
✔️ Keep your phone and apps updated to avoid security vulnerabilities.
✔️ Log out from shared/public computers (library, cyber café).
✔️ Adjust privacy settings on social media to limit what strangers can see.


Don’ts (Avoid these risky behaviors):

❌ Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, or passwords with anyone—even if they claim to be from a bank.
❌ Don’t accept unknown UPI “collect requests” or scan random QR codes.
❌ Avoid using public Wi-Fi for banking or sensitive logins.
❌ Don’t download files or apps from unknown or untrusted sources.
❌ Don’t overshare personal details (address, college ID, phone number) online.
