Do’s

1.Think before posting – ensure your content is respectful and appropriate
2.Maintain privacy settings – regularly check and update your account security
3.Share credible information – verify facts before reposting or sharing
4.Build a positive digital presence – post content that reflects your skills and interests
5.Respect others online – be polite and avoid arguments or offensive language


❌ Don’ts

1.Don’t share personal/sensitive information (address, phone number, passwords)
2.Don’t engage in cyberbullying or trolling
3.Don’t believe or spread fake news blindly
4.Don’t overshare daily activities that may risk your safety
5.Don’t post inappropriate or offensive content that can harm your reputation
